# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Developers who use AI coding tools and want to see their remaining included allowance and paid continuation capacity at a glance. They should not need to open each provider, interpret raw quota or billing data, or trust a third-party service with account tokens.

## Product Purpose

Ammo turns Claude, Codex, and Cursor usage into a native, glanceable iOS utility. Success means the current included allowance, reset timing, on-demand balance or spending control, and account identity are immediately legible, current, and trustworthy while credentials remain on-device.

## Brand Personality

Native, precise, quiet. Ammo should feel like a first-party iOS utility: familiar enough to disappear into the Home Screen, but polished enough that the data feels intentional rather than improvised.

## Anti-references

No icon soup, invented provider marks, logos boxed inside unnecessary tiles, generic dashboard chrome, cramped or clipped content, decorative visual noise, or styling that fights iOS appearance modes.

## Design Principles

- Make the remaining allowance readable in one glance.
- Keep included quota and monetary on-demand capacity visibly separate.
- Preserve personal, team, and organization scopes instead of collapsing them.
- Use official provider identity without added decoration.
- Follow iOS typography, spacing, and appearance conventions.
- Preserve information hierarchy across every widget family and device size.
- Treat reliability and honest data states as part of the visual design.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Respect automatic light and dark appearance, Clear and tinted widget rendering, sufficient contrast, legible system typography, and semantic color that is never the only carrier of meaning. Layout must remain valid at the system-provided widget size without clipping or invalid geometry.
